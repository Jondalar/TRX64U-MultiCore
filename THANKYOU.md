# Thank you

TRX64U MultiCore stands on the work of others. Who wrote which file, under which licence, is recorded in
`PROVENANCE.md` and in each core's `cores/<name>/rtl/NOTICE`; this page says thank you.

## The host and the C64

- **Gideon Zweijtzer**, for the whole Ultimate project: the 1541 Ultimate, the Ultimate 64 and the
  C64 Ultimate, their firmware and FPGA blocks, all open under the GPL.
- **Christian Gleissner**, for his tools around the Ultimate
  ([1541ultimate-tools](https://github.com/chrisgleissner/1541ultimate-tools)).
- **Dag Lem**, for reDIP SID and reDIP CIA ([reDIP-SID](https://github.com/daglem/reDIP-SID),
  [reDIP-CIA](https://github.com/daglem/reDIP-CIA)).
- **Mateusz Nalewajski (m1nl)**, for icepi-zero-c64 ([icepi-zero-c64](https://github.com/m1nl/icepi-zero-c64)),
  the C64 core this one grew from.
- **Sameer Puri**, for the HDMI encoder ([hdl-util/hdmi](https://github.com/hdl-util/hdmi)), and **nand2mario**,
  for the USB HID host.
- **The VICE team, Groepaz above all**, for the many tools, the test programs and the inspiration.

## The cores

- **Till Harbaum and the MiSTle-Dev projects** ([MiSTle-Dev](https://github.com/MiSTle-Dev)): NanoMig, NanoApple2,
  C16Nano and VIC20Nano, the trees the Amiga, Apple //e, C264 and VC-20 cores come from.
- **Dennis van Weeren, Jakub Bednarski, Rok Krajnc, Alexey Melnikov and Tobias Gubener**, for Minimig and Minimig-AGA;
  **Tobias Gubener** also for TG68K, the 68020 of the Amiga 1200.
- **Jorge Cwik**, for FX68K, the 68000 of the Amiga and the Neo Geo.
- **Szombathelyi Gyorgy and Stephen A. Edwards**, for the Apple //e machine; **Peter Wendrich** and **A. Daly**, for
  its 65C02.
- **Istvan Hegedus**, for FPGATED, and **Jozsef Laszlo**, for its colour table (C264).
- **Mike Johnson (MikeJ) and Wolfgang Scherr (WoS)**, for the VC-20 and the 6561, and with **Daniel Wallner** and the
  FPGAARCADE crew for T65 and T80, the 6502 and Z80 of several cores.
- **Mark Watson**, for the Atari 800 XL core.
- **Sorgelig and the MiSTer-devel team**, for the Amstrad CPC, NES, ZX Spectrum, SNES and Neo Geo cores;
  **Ludvig Strigeus**, for the NES; **srg320**, for the SNES; **Sean 'Furrtek' Gonsalves**, for the Neo Geo chips;
  **Jose Tejada (jotego)**, for jt10 and jt49, the Neo Geo's sound.
- **The ZX Spectrum Next team**: Victor Trucco, Fabio Belavenuto, Alvin Albrecht, Garry Lancaster, Charlie Ingley
  and the others.
