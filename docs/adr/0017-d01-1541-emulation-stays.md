# 17. 1541 emulation stays

Date: 2026-10-05

Status: Accepted

Formerly: decision 1 (docs/architecture-reset-2026-10-05.md). Old references to it resolve here.

## Context

The architecture reset asked whether to keep or drop the 1541 emulation to save LUTs.

## Decision

Alex 2026-10-05: **stays** ("eigentlich gehörte die 1581 auch dazu"; the 1581 stays T56, M2).

## Consequences

The software-floppy alternative was rejected (ADR-0023). Drive options (mm_drive for A, A+B) are T56 (M2).
