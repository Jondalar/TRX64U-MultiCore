# 25. Ring and still-picture formats are free to optimise

Date: 2026-10-06

Status: Accepted

Formerly: decision 7. Old references to it resolve here.

## Context

Ring, still and record formats were kept compatible with the TRX64 emulator.

## Decision

Alex 2026-10-06: free to optimise from our point of view; they need not match the TRX64 emulator - only we and Mike use both, Mike hardly yet. Exchange with TRX64 stays M2/M3 (A09).

## Consequences

Formats can change; the exchange with TRX64 (.c64re, board snapshot with SID/drive/cart) is rebuilt in T66.
