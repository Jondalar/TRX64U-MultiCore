# T77: trxmon over RPC: `bk` + `g` hangs the app

Milestone M2, status 📅. Open (one line, as in the TODO table): WiFi part in T78

## Description

Found 2026-10-10 evening on RC1 (integ-m1 692f643d, app 3c8e4aa, trxmon 64ee9b9e) while recording the video: over the WebSocket
JSON-RPC (`monitor/exec`, TCP 4312) the sequence `pause`, `z`, `r`, `rstep 20`, `r`, `bk 1298`, `g` (PC was $1298, Sam's Journey
GMod2 CRT running, the Freeze menu open on the C64 screen) got no answer within 20 s; then the board stopped answering
altogether: no ping, no REST, port 4312 closed, the console stopped printing. Only a power cycle helped. The same session's
`pause`, `r`, `d`, `m`, `io`, `rewind`, `chis`, `whowrote`, `rstep` (mid-instruction refusal), `run` answered. Not analysed.

- **2026-10-10 night: the WiFi part is its own task, T78 (Alex: "Mach ein todo und eine spec für uns intern. M2").** Spec docs/specs/T78-wifi-rpc-timeout.md.
- **2026-10-10 night, cause narrowed and trxmon fixed (Claude; Alex: "Mach den trxmon fix. Und der wifi fix wäre ja esp32. Da wollen wir doch eh dran.").** Not reproduced in 19 tries (one at a time, batched, trxmon from the menu or as `serve`, with a concurrent REST serve request) with a JTAG watcher armed. The original console showed `^~^~^~` before it went quiet: `^` is the WiFi DMA UART without a free receive buffer (`dma_uart.cc:179`, then BufReq off), `~` unsolicited WiFi packets (`wifi_cmd.cc:26`): the tasks that free those buffers did not run. trxmon runs above the network tasks (`main.c:450`) and its stop waits only yielded (`mach.c` `wait_status`, `plat_yield` = `delay_ms(0)`), up to 2 s each, chained in replays. Gideon's WiFi RPC waits for the ESP32's reply without a timeout (`wifi_cmd.h` `TRANSMIT`: `xTaskNotifyWait(..., portMAX_DELAY)`); a lost reply blocks the calling task for good, and in lwIP's thread that is the whole network. WiFi was on (since the settings restore of T49). Fix in trxmon (app 1791f3c5): `breathe()` sleeps PLAT_SLEEP_MIN_MS every 20 ms of waiting; host test (fails without the fix): the long `rstep` replay sleeps; host suites 1077/279/74/29 checks, 0 failed. Board (new trxmon over FTP into /Flash/apps): 15 stress rounds pass, REST latency probe during them below. The WiFi timeout goes with the ESP32 work (Alex); note: the wait without a timeout is on our side of the SLIP RPC, in the Ultimate app (`software/io/wifi/wifi_cmd.h`), not in the ESP32 firmware; T26 (docs/boot-chain-concept.md) rebuilds exactly that RPC path.
