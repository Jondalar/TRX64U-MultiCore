# T76: R21 (`CIA_RSTn`): what Gideon's design resets with it

Milestone M2, status 📅. Open (one line, as in the TODO table): Not measured

## Description

R21 (`CIA_RSTn`) is an FPGA ball with a pull-up. Measured 2026-10-02 (SAMPLE, Gideon's bitstream, EF3): while the EF3's reset
button is held, P20 (port RESET sense) reads 1 and Gideon's design drives R21 low. Our bitstream never drives R21 (T16 §13.6);
it resets the C64 through the P20 sense, and the EF3 reset button works on our core (Alex, 2026-10-10). Unknown: what R21
resets on the board (the CIA side of the port, by its name) and whether it feeds back into P20. Tool: debug bitstream branch
`t16-r21-dbg` (`tests/cart/r21_probe.py`, CI 261, old base).
