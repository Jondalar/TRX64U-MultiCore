# trx64u row audit (2026-11-18)

Every row of the trx64u core menu (branch t228-int at 194bc959d) checked against the pinned TRX64-Ultimate bitstream
(designs/c64_basic of main-31e69921, release 0.3.0) and its firmware, with the evidence per row; every verdict other than
WORKS was verified a second time. Spec: [T228](../specs/T228-trx64u-generic-menu.md) §5. Read this before checking a trx64u
row again; TRX64-Ultimate's own analyses are `docs/gap-audit-gideon-app.md` and `docs/gideon-menu-tree.md` there.

Paths: MC = this repository, TU = TRX64-Ultimate `designs/c64_basic`, FW/UFW = TRX64-Ultimate's firmware
(`software/ultimate-app/software`), IC = its include files. Line numbers are those of the audited commits.

Verdicts: WORKS (the row does what it says on this bitstream), PARTIAL (works with a gap, see fix), FAKE (reaches no
register or service: the 49 FAKE rows of menu.toml left it on t228-int in 4383257dd).

Totals: 338 rows, 228 WORKS, 58 PARTIAL, 52 FAKE; 8 rows missing (below).
