# cores/c64scpu provenance (T182)

Spec: `docs/specs/T182-c64scpu-core.md` (T1 base, §5 build notes). The core is a fork of `cores/c128` (step 1 of `docs/todo/T182.md`): the C128's
flat large-core tree with the VDC, the Z80, the MMU 8722, the C128 address map, the VIC-IIe registers, the extra keys and the 2 MHz bus cycles cut out, so that
what is left is a plain C64 with its 6510. Third-party files keep their headers and are TREX-internal until the licence question is settled (T113 Q9, which
applies here as it did to the C128 core). `cores/c128/PROVENANCE.md` has the upstream sources of the C128 core; none of its MiSTer C128 files (`vdc/`, `z80/`,
`c128_mmu8722.vhd`) is in this tree.

## Copies of cores/c128 (and through it of cores/c64u, vendor/icepi)
- `vicii_c64scpu.v`: copy of `cores/c128/vicii_c128.v`, which is `vendor/icepi/vicii-kawari/hdl/vicii.v` (Kawari, GPL-3.0-or-later) with one change: the dot phase
  (`dot_rising`) stops while `stall` is up (T113 5.4).
- `c64_bus_arbiter_c64scpu.v`: `cores/c128/c64_bus_arbiter_c128.v` (a copy of `vendor/icepi/c64_bus_arbiter.v`, GPL-3.0-or-later) with the C128 map, the second RAM
  block, the VDC and VIC-IIe selects and the 2 MHz cycles taken out again: the PLA of `vendor/icepi/c64_pla.v` in place, the ROM image as 4 KiB banks (BASIC banks
  0/1, KERNAL banks 2/3), `ram_vic` / `vic_bank_tag` for the RAM path, and a machine reset that stops the CPU's half only (T113 5.6).
- `c64_top_c64scpu.v`, `c64_core_c64scpu.v`, `layer2_c64scpu.v`, `dma_win_c64scpu.v`: copies of the C128 files (which are copies of the `cores/c64u` files, cut down and
  extended) with the C128 parts cut. In step 1 the CPU was `vendor/icepi/cpu6510.v` in place (the C128 copy `cpu6510_c128.v` only added the 8502's port bit 6 and the 2 MHz clock
  enable; neither was needed); since step 2 it is the 65C816 below, and the 6510 is only built with `C64SCPU_CPU6510` (the reference of `sim/run_emu.sh`). The cold start (`COLD`), the frame measurement and the ROM line cache hold (`rom_hold`) are the C128 core's. **Fixed in the copy:**
  `rom_wait_q` / `rom_hold` are declared outside the `ifdef Z80` they were in (`cores/c128/c64_top_c128.v:617-655` did not elaborate with the ROM cache and without the
  Z80: `ERROR: [Synth 8-36] 'rom_hold' is not declared`, T182 §3 T1).
- `c64_mem_c64scpu.v`, `c64scpu_ramd.v`, `c64scpu_romc.v`, `c64scpu_ddrx.v`: copies of `c64_mem_c128d.v`, `c128_ramd.v`, `c128_romc.v`, `c128_ddrx.v` (T140 D25/D27): the RAM
  in the DDR2 behind a slot and line cache, the ROM image behind a line cache. RAM block 1 of `c64scpu_ramd.v` is never addressed here; step 3 of the plan replaces
  this memory path.
- `c64scpu_chr.v`: `c128_chr.v` cut to one 4 KiB set (the C64 character ROM). `c64scpu_regs.v`: the register page of `c128_regs.v` with the C128 registers cut
  (identity `SCPU`). `c64scpu_joyswap.v`: `c128_joyswap.v` (T157). `core_sources.tcl`, `c64_core_ooc.xdc`: the C128's, adapted. No logo yet (step 8 of the plan).
- `mono/`: the flat build. `build_mono.tcl`, `build.sh`, `core.toml`, `top.v` (+ `top.v.diff`), `c64scpu_avout.v` / `c64scpu_yc.v` / `c64scpu_avpins.v` (the A/V port, T140;
  `c64scpu_yc.v` is derived from `vendor/mister/yc_out.sv`, GPL-2.0-or-later), `avid_pins.xdc`, `cache_cfg_pkg.vhd`, `stubs/` are the C128 core's with the VDC, Z80 and
  frame-writer parts removed; `mono/host/` is this core's own copy of the host files (T164 3a Q1; `mono/host/FROM` says which).
- Unmodified files are read in place from `vendor/icepi`, `vendor/mister`, `cores/c64u`, `cores/c1541`; nothing there is edited.

## The 65C816 (T182 step 2)
- `cpu/*.vhd`: generated by `tools/scpu_vendor816.py` (`--check` in the lint stage) from `cores/snes/rtl/65C816` (SNES_MiSTer, GPL-3.0; provenance and licence: `cores/snes/rtl/NOTICE`, `cores/snes/rtl/FROM`).
  Differences from the upstream files are listed in the script's header: entity names prefixed `s816_`, lower case ports, the added `e_out` (emulation flag), `when others => null` made `when others =>`
  in complete cases (the fix `tools/snes_gen.py` makes for GHDL), the microcode ROM as one flat 2048 x 53 bit array, and five changes of behaviour: the XCE branch of X, Y
  and SP gated with EN (T182 step 3a), bit 4 (B) = 0 in the P byte an IRQ or NMI pushes in emulation mode (T182), and in emulation mode the modify cycle of a
  read-modify-write instruction writes the byte read, as the 6510 and VICE's W65C816S do (T241), and RTI in emulation mode takes 6 cycles, one internal cycle more than upstream, as
  the W65C816S does (T241 S2), and a taken branch that stays in its page decides IRQ and NMI one cycle earlier in emulation mode, as the 6510 does (T241 D4). Nothing in
  `cores/snes` is edited.
- `scpu_pport.v`: the 6510 port `$00/$01` out of `vendor/icepi/cpu6510.v` (Copyright 2026 Mateusz Nalewajski, GPL-3.0-or-later), without the turbo and state-bus paths.

## Ours (written for this project)
- `cpu65816_c64scpu.v`: the bus adaptor of the 65C816 to the C64's PHI2 path (T182 step 2, spec §5.4). `sim/run_emu.sh`, `sim/check_emu.py`, `tools/gen_c64scpu_emu.py`: the emulation-mode bench.
- `sim/`: the boot bench (`tb_c64scpu.v`, `build_machine.sh`, `run_base.sh`, `check_screen.py`, `mk_tb_ports.py`, `run_real.sh`); `tools/gen_c64scpu_test.py` (the synthetic ROM) and
  `tools/c64scpu_rom_image.py` (the image of real ROMs for the local bench). The testbench is a cut of `cores/c128/sim/tb_c128.v`.
- `trial/`: the area trial of T182 §5.1 (synthesis only, run by hand; it builds `cores/c128`, not this tree).

No ROM byte is in this tree. The SuperCPU's ROM (T182 D2) is supplied by the person and never enters the repository, a release or a `.cfw`.
